# Target house — build reference

The real house this designer is modeling (aerial front elevation photo). Drop the
photo next to this file as `target-house.jpg` to keep the literal image with the notes.

## Overall
- Two-story main body, long rectangular footprint, steep gable roof running the length.
- Dark reddish-brown stained **horizontal lap siding** (the "Wood plank / sideways boards" texture).
- **Standing-seam metal roof**, dark charcoal-brown (the "Metal roof" texture), on every roof surface.
- **White-trimmed windows** with a center mullion (matches the optional window-frame toggle).

## Roof features
- Main roof: steep gable, metal standing-seam, big overhang.
- **Two front gabled dormers** on the upper floor, each with a white-framed window (already supported via the per-wall dormer flag).
- **Two skylights** flush in the main roof slope.
- Metal **flue/chimney pipe** poking through the left part of the roof.

## The next challenge — smaller roof segments (secondary roofs)
Three sub-roofs that are NOT the main roof or dormers:
1. **Entryway roof (left):** a single-story wing on the left with its own lower roof over a
   row of small ribbon windows.
2. **Bay-window roof (middle-front):** a small hip/gable roof capping the projecting bay
   (the angled bay we already model), sitting below the main eave.
3. **Porch roof (right):** a small shed/lean-to roof over the patio/porch on the right end.

These are lower, local roofs attached to a portion of the footprint or to a wall — distinct
from the whole-house roof and from dormers. Design approach is under discussion.

## Windows / walls
- Large ground-floor sliding windows, white frames, 2-pane (center mullion).
- Bay has wrap-around windows on its angled faces.
- Upper floor: dormer windows + skylights.
